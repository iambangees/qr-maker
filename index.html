2 · WhatsApp link maker

Type a number and a message and get a wa.me link — tap it and WhatsApp opens straight into a chat with that person, message already written. Handy for shop numbers, flyers and your own contact details.

Everything on this page runs in your browser. The QR code is drawn on your device from the text you type — nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and the page works offline once loaded. QR codes are black on white with a quiet zone on purpose: that is what phone cameras read most reliably, so the colours are fixed. A very long link still scans, but a short link scans faster — that is why the presets use short portfolio links.
